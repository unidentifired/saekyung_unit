Privacy & storage on this site

This is a small, independently owned booking site for two condo units. Here's plainly what happens with your data when you use it.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies

This site doesn't run ad networks or analytics scripts, and doesn't set tracking cookies of its own.

What browser storage is used, and why

When you sign in with Google to leave a review, Firebase (Google's platform, which powers this site's backend) uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. This is essential storage — the review form can't work without it — not an advertising or tracking mechanism.

What we store about you

If you leave a review, we store your name and photo as provided by your Google account, your star rating, and your written comment. This is used only to display your review (once approved) and to let you manage or remove it later on request.

Who to contact

To ask about your data, or to request a review be removed, reach out via the contact details on the main site.
